What I do

Website Security Checks
for Businesses

I reach out to businesses — or they reach out to me — and I run a thorough security check on their website using professional tools. You get a clear report on what I found and what you should do about it.

Website Security Check

I scan your website with a range of security tools to uncover vulnerabilities — things like exposed admin pages, outdated software, misconfigured headers, SSL issues, and common attack entry points that most businesses never know about until it's too late.

After the scan I put together a straightforward report that explains what I found, how serious each issue is, and what steps you can take to fix it. No confusing jargon, just clear information you can act on.

Request a Check →

SSL & HTTPS Check

Making sure your connection is encrypted and your certificate is valid and correctly configured.

Exposed Pages & Login Panels

Checking for publicly accessible admin areas, login pages, or sensitive directories that shouldn't be visible.

Security Headers

Reviewing HTTP headers that protect against common attacks like clickjacking, cross-site scripting, and more.

Outdated Software

Identifying CMS versions, plugins, or libraries that are out of date and known to have security vulnerabilities.

Clear Written Report

A plain-language summary of everything found, with a severity rating and actionable steps for each issue.

A note on scope

Orbinex Security is a one-person operation. I don't manage your infrastructure or provide ongoing monitoring — what I do is give you a detailed, honest look at how your website holds up from a security standpoint. Think of it as a health check, not a managed service. That transparency is something I take seriously.

How it works

From First Contact to Clear Findings

1

2

About Orbinex Security

Built Around Clear Security

Orbinex Security is being built around a straightforward idea: smaller businesses should be able to understand the security of their website without needing to decode a wall of technical jargon.

The name reflects that idea. Orb represents the digital world — the cloud, the systems, the infrastructure that modern business depends on. Nex stands for nexus: connection, network, the invisible threads that bind it all together. Orbinex Security is here to protect those connections.

Based in Belgium, Orbinex is focused on practical website security checks, transparent scope, and clear reporting as the project grows.
