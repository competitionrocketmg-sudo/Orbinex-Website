Privacy Policy

Orbinex Security values transparency and privacy. This page explains how personal information may be handled when you contact or interact with Orbinex Security.

1. Who is responsible for your data?

Orbinex Security is a Belgium-based cybersecurity venture. For privacy-related questions, you can contact orbinex.cybersecurity@gmail.com .

2. What information may be collected?

When you contact Orbinex Security, information you voluntarily provide may be processed, such as:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your company or organisation
  • Information included in your message or enquiry

3. Why is this information used?

Personal information is used only where necessary to respond to enquiries, discuss requested services, communicate with you and provide relevant information about an assessment.

4. Data retention

Personal information is kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was provided or where retention is required by applicable law.

5. Sharing of information

Orbinex Security does not sell personal information. Information may only be processed by services necessary to operate the website or communicate with you, where applicable.

6. Cookies and analytics

The current Orbinex Security website does not intentionally use advertising or tracking cookies. If analytics or additional third-party services are introduced in the future, this policy may be updated accordingly.

7. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable data protection law, you may have rights regarding your personal data, including requesting access, correction or deletion of information associated with you.

Requests can be sent to orbinex.cybersecurity@gmail.com .

8. Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the website, services or data-processing practices change.
